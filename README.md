Sum up the world news of **Wednesday, 25 November 2026** in one image.

## The image

`entry.png` — 1024 × 1024, 46 stories across 9 regions, plus the day's
markets. The top story: Houthi strikes on a Saudi oil refinery and the
Riyadh financial district. Regions: North America, Latin America, Europe,
Middle East, Africa, South Asia, East Asia, Southeast Asia, Oceania.

Sources checked for the date: Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, SCMP, NHK, Japan Times,
Al Jazeera, Le Monde — all stories dated 25 November 2026.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when set).
Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output. No network.
